## Verified headings and mapping use

| Article | Verified heading | Engineering evidence this skill maps |
|---|---|---|
| Art. 5 | Principles relating to processing of personal data | Purpose limitation, minimisation, storage limitation |
| Art. 6 | Lawfulness of processing | Recorded basis and processing decision |
| Art. 7 | Conditions for consent | Versioned consent and withdrawal events |
| Art. 15 | Right of access by the data subject | Scoped export of actual stored and derived data |
| Art. 17 | Right to erasure ('right to be forgotten') | Deletion, expiry, or documented exception per copy |
| Art. 20 | Right to data portability | Structured machine-readable export |
| Art. 25 | Data protection by design and by default | Default minimisation and protective configuration |
| Art. 28 | Processor | Processor inventory and instructions |
| Art. 30 | Records of processing activities | Generated data inventory and processing record |
| Art. 32 | Security of processing | Risk-matched technical and organisational measures |
| Art. 33 | Notification of a personal data breach to the supervisory authority | Incident evidence and timeline support |
| Art. 34 | Communication of a personal data breach to the data subject | Impact and affected-population evidence |
| Art. 35 | Data protection impact assessment | Documented risk assessment where applicable |

Article 32(1) examples verified from the fetched article text include pseudonymisation and
 encryption; ongoing confidentiality, integrity, availability and resilience; timely restoration
of access after an incident; and regular testing, assessment, and evaluation of effectiveness.

These mappings describe technical support, not a legal conclusion. Whether an exemption, lawful
basis, notification duty, or processor role applies depends on facts and counsel.

## Deliberate omissions

No GDPR deadline, exception, or paragraph beyond the Article 32(1) examples is asserted here
unless it appears in the table or the skill's implementation record. Re-check the current text
before quoting in an external report.
