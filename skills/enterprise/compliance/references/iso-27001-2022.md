## Mapping caution

An Annex A control title does not establish applicability or implementation. Record the Statement
of Applicability decision separately. Map evidence only to the scoped system, environment,
population, and period that produced it.

Do not say that a CI artifact "meets ISO 27001." Say it provides evidence for the named technical
control. Certification depends on the management system and certification body assessment.
